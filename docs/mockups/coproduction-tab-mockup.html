What to actually reserve
— Each asking price × its likelihood, added up

Expected cost, not committed cost.

We will not win every one of these, so reserving the full asking total would lock up money we never spend. Multiplying each asking price by its likelihood and adding the results gives a realistic FY27 number: a $1M title at 25% contributes $250,000 to the reserve.

It is a planning figure for the budget line, never a number to quote to a partner.
